About Amara Trading

Who We Are

Amara Trading is an emerging leader in the supply and trade of goods, commodities, raw materials, and foodstuffs from Syria to global markets. We connect producers with international buyers, fostering growth, reliability, and sustainable trade worldwide.

Our Operations

Our operations span the full supply chain — extraction, trucking, and maritime transport — ensuring efficiency, reliability, and excellence. We source petroleum and energy products, sulphur, industrial minerals, and building materials with exceptional purity and consistency, managed through a fully integrated logistics network.

Our Values

We prioritize transparency, quality assurance, and mutual success. We build long-term partnerships with producers, manufacturers, and international buyers across the energy, minerals, and industrial sectors, committed to reliable supply and industrial advancement.

Quality Philosophy

Quality is our standard, not a promise. Through strict quality control, technical rigor, and responsive customer support, we deliver commodities that meet international standards and ensure sustainable results.

Where We Ship

Global Shipping Network

We serve customers worldwide with reliable commodity shipments — from petroleum products to minerals and building materials — moving from Mediterranean ports to destinations across the globe.

Logistics

Supply Chain Excellence

Our end‑to‑end logistics ensure predictable delivery from mine to vessel while maintaining strict quality control throughout the entire supply chain.

Incoterms & Logistics

Established mine-to-port network under government and private management. Available on FOB & CIF terms.

Quality Verification

Independent inspection by SGS or equivalent to ensure product quality and consistency.

Shipment Volume

Typical vessel capacity: 20,000–40,000 tons per shipment.

Additional Offer Details

  • Certificate of Origin issued by the relevant government authority.
  • Site visits to the mine, port, and relevant authorities can be arranged post-contract.
  • For phosphate rock shipments specifically: buyers must be fertilizer manufacturers or distributors and confirm agricultural end-use in writing, as required by Syrian export regulations. Terms for other commodities are confirmed per contract.
Ports

Port Operations

Experienced in bulk handling at Mediterranean ports to ensure safe loading and fast vessel turnaround.

We coordinate stow plans, draft surveys, and sampling. Documentation and export facilitation are handled in‑house for speed and compliance.
