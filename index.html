Our Products

Phosphate Rock – Grade G4

P2O5 29.3% – 30.2%
BPL 64% – 66%

Grade G4 phosphate rock offers high purity and consistent composition, making it ideal for the production of high-grade phosphoric acid and compound fertilizers. Its elevated P2O5 levels ensure strong reactivity and improved yield efficiency in both agricultural and industrial processing. Material is screened, low in impurities, and suitable for direct acidulation or further beneficiation.

Phosphate Rock – Grade G6

P2O5 28.38% – 29.29%
BPL 62% – 63%

Grade G6 provides a stable and reliable phosphate source for blended fertilizers and general industrial use. It maintains uniform grain size and favorable solubility characteristics, allowing smooth handling and predictable reaction performance in phosphoric acid and fertilizer manufacturing operations.
